Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 30, 2026

This policy explains what personal information All Scope Holdings LLC ("we", "us") collects through allscopeholdingsllc.com and our services, how we use it and the choices you have.

1. Information we collect

  • Booking information: your name, e-mail, phone number, address and the details you give us when you book a call through our Cal.com calendar or contact us.
  • E-mail sign-ups: your e-mail address when you request our $10-off code.
  • Payment information: payments are processed by Stripe. We receive confirmation of payment and limited details (such as the last 4 digits of your card), but never your full card number.
  • Device information: during a repair we may see data stored on your device. We only access what's needed to repair and test it, and we never copy or keep your personal files unless you ask us to (for example, for a data transfer).
  • Website data: our site does not use advertising or analytics cookies. It stores a small value in your browser to remember that you have already seen our discount pop-up.

2. How we use it

  • To schedule and provide the services you request, send quotes, invoices and appointment reminders.
  • To answer your questions and provide warranty support.
  • To send you offers and tips if you signed up — you can unsubscribe at any time.
  • To comply with legal, tax and accounting obligations.

3. Who we share it with

We do not sell your personal information. We share it only with service providers that help us run the business, under their own privacy terms:

  • Cal.com (appointment booking)
  • Stripe (payment processing)
  • FormSubmit (delivery of e-mail sign-ups to our inbox)
  • Google (e-mail hosting)
  • GitHub Pages (website hosting)

We may also disclose information if required by law. Links to Amazon in our Shop take you to Amazon's website, which is governed by Amazon's privacy notice.

4. How long we keep it

We keep booking and invoice records for as long as needed to provide our services, honor warranties and meet tax and legal requirements (generally up to 5 years). E-mail sign-ups are kept until you unsubscribe.

5. Your choices and rights

You can ask us to access, correct or delete your personal information, or to stop sending you marketing e-mails, by contacting us at support@allscopeholdingsllc.com. We will respond within 30 days.

6. Security

We use reputable providers and reasonable measures to protect your information. No method of transmission or storage is 100% secure, but we work to keep your data safe.

7. Children

Our services are not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their personal information.

8. GPC Studio

If you use GPC Studio (gpcstudio.app), we also process your account details (name, e-mail), your subscription status and the content you create, in order to provide the service. Payments are handled by Stripe.

9. Changes

We may update this policy. The "Last updated" date shows the latest version.
