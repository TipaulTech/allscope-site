FAQ

Frequently asked questions

How much does a repair cost?

It depends on the device and the part. Phone screen repairs start at $79. Every visit starts with a $29 diagnostic, which is deducted from the price of your repair. You always get a clear quote before we start, and nothing is done without your OK.

How long does a repair take?

Many phone and computer repairs are done the same day. If a part has to be ordered, it usually takes 2–5 business days — we keep you updated the whole time.

Do you come to me?

Yes. We work on site at your home or office across Broward and Miami-Dade counties for installations and most repairs.

Is there a warranty?

Every repair comes with a 90-day limited warranty on the parts we install and our labor. It doesn't cover new physical or liquid damage after the repair. See our Refund & Warranty policy.

What happens to my data?

We only access what's needed to repair and test your device. We still recommend backing up before any repair — and we can do the backup or data transfer for you.

How can I pay?

We accept major credit and debit cards through secure online checkout, sent after your quote is approved.

Can I cancel or reschedule?

Yes — free of charge up to 24 hours before your appointment, from your booking confirmation e-mail or by calling (954) 449-3719.
